# Code of Conduct

DailyKhata welcomes respectful collaboration among team members, reviewers and other contributors. This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and project-related team discussions.

## Expected conduct

- Treat others with respect regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.
- Critique work constructively, explain concerns and respond to feedback in good faith.
- Share knowledge, give fair credit and communicate workload or deadline difficulties early.
- Respect privacy, academic integrity and the ownership of contributed material.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, plagiarism and disclosure of private information without consent are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is also unacceptable.

## Raising a concern

Team members should contact a trusted, uninvolved teammate privately through their existing university or team contact channel. If the concern involves that person or cannot be resolved within the team, contact the supervisor through the established university channel.

External contributors may use a contact route a maintainer has voluntarily published on their GitHub profile. If none is available, open an issue asking only for a private contact route, without naming affected people or describing the incident publicly. Personal contact details are intentionally not published here.

Share only the information needed to understand the concern. Reports should be handled discreetly and shared only with people needed to resolve them; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. A person involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome.

## Response

An uninvolved maintainer should listen to those affected, consider the available evidence and explain the outcome privately. Responses may include a request to change behaviour, a warning, moderation of harmful content or referral to the supervisor. Serious or repeated misconduct may lead repository owners to restrict participation. Academic concerns should follow the university's applicable procedures.
