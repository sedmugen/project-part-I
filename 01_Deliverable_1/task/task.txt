To identify the intended users of the proposed system and understand their needs, problems, and expectations. This activity will help students define appropriate requirements and develop a solution that addresses real user problems.

Students are required to submit a document containing the following:

Identify the overall market/audience for the proposed solution

Identify primary users and secondary users of the system

Describe the problems faced by users and their expectations from the proposed system

Explain why this target market is suitable and relevant for the proposed solution.
