Deliverable 2: Problem Identification, Stakeholder Identification & Requirement Elicitation  

Students are required to submit the following three deliverables as part of their project development process. The submission must be project-specific and evidence-based. Simply providing definitions or theoretical explanations is not sufficient.

1. Problem Identification
Students must clearly identify and define the problem that their proposed project intends to address.
The deliverable must include:
A clear description of the identified problem.
The context/situation in which the problem exists.
Existing process/system and its limitations, where applicable.
The method/approach used to identify the problem, such as:
Observation
Interviews
Survey/Questionnaire
Literature/Market Research
Existing System Analysis
User Feedback
A clear explanation of how the selected method was actually used to identify the problem.Evidence/findings obtained through the selected method.A well-defined and concise Problem Statement.
Note: Students must explain what they actually did to identify the problem. Simply stating “the problem was identified through observation/interview” is not sufficient.

Problem Statement of maximum 4 Lines.

2. Stakeholder Identification
Students must identify and analyse all relevant stakeholders associated with their project.
The following analysis is mandatory:Industry → Market → Market Segment → User Profile → Persona → Stakeholders
Students must clearly identify:
Industry: The industry/domain in which the proposed solution will operate.
Market: The relevant market within that industry.
Market Segment: The specific segment being targeted.
User Profile: Characteristics, needs, behaviour, expectations, technical knowledge, and other relevant attributes of the target users.
Persona: A representative user persona based on the identified user profile. The persona should include relevant information such as:
Role/Occupation
Goals
Needs
Pain Points
Behaviour
Expectations
Other Stakeholders: Any additional stakeholders such as administrators, business owners, managers, service providers, organisations, regulatory bodies, etc., where applicable.
For each stakeholder, students must explain their role, interest, needs, and impact on the proposed system.

A Table Having all above parameters.

3. Requirement Elicitation
Students must identify the Requirement Elicitation Technique(s) used to collect requirements for their proposed system.
Possible techniques include:
Interviews
Surveys/Questionnaires
Observation
Focus Groups
Brainstorming
Document Analysis
Existing System Analysis
Workshops
Prototyping
User Feedback/User Stories
For each technique used, students must explain:Which technique was used?Why was this technique selected?Who/which stakeholders or users were involved?How was the technique conducted?What information/findings were obtained?How were the findings converted into system requirements?

Important: Students must provide evidence of how the elicitation technique was applied to their project. Merely defining an elicitation technique is not acceptable.Expected Outcome
